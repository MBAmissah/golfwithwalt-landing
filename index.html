Fair questions

I'm in my seventies and stiff as a fence post. Is this for me?
Especially. Every technique in the book assumes reduced rotation and honest joints — and where standard instruction fights senior comfort, comfort wins. Part III exists precisely for backs, knees, and hands.

Will this add 30 yards like the videos promise?
No, and I won't pretend otherwise. Most golfers find smoother tempo and cleaner contact give some distance back — how much is yours to discover. What the book will change immediately is how many shots you waste. That's where scores live.

Do I need a gym, a launch monitor, or new clubs?
No. You need a pencil, ten minutes here and there, and maybe $30 of honest hardware over time — the book tells you exactly which small purchases matter and which don't. No brands, ever.
